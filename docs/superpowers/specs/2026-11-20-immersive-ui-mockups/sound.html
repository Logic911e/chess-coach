Which sounds would you want to hear for an hour?

Three sound palettes. Press the buttons to hear each event, then click the card you like. Keep your volume low to start. They are all quiet on purpose, and there would be a mute toggle plus a volume slider.

A · Wood — real-board clicks

Dry, woody taps like pieces on a physical board. Heavier thud for captures. A dull low tone for blunders.

B · Felt — muted and padded

Very soft, rounded thuds, like a felt-topped board. The least tiring over a long session.

C · Chime — glassy and musical

Clean little pings and short melodic phrases. Best moves rise, blunders fall. More playful, more "app".
